Why an AI employee, not an agent

You build an agent, engineer it, troubleshoot it, and maintain it.

You onboard an employee, train it, and give it feedback.

In a regulated operation that difference decides whether the thing survives its first exception.

A copilot waits to be asked. An agent waits to be maintained. An AI employee owns the outcome.
